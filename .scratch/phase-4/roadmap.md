# Phase 4 roadmap — the learning-engine season board

Status: planned (opened 2026-11-11; owner nomination「与 Synapse 进行特定功能的深度对比、提取掌握门控的设计，形成一个新的 phase 来实施」)
Milestone: Phase 4 — learning-engine season

This file is the phase-level index over `.scratch/phase-4/epic-*/`. Phase 4 turns Synapse's Mastery Score from a **display** into a **learning engine**: gates that consume the score, review that adapts, grading that refuses to guess. The design source is [deeptutor-comparison.md](deeptutor-comparison.md) (HKUDS/DeepTutor v1.7.2 extraction, 2026-11-11) — every epic grills against that doc's §3 mechanics and §5 mapping, and imports **design, not code**. Its UI/UX companion piece is [deeptutor-uiux-comparison.md](deeptutor-uiux-comparison.md) (nav/flows, same date) — epic-29 consumes its §5.3-5.4; epic-33 was extracted from its §5.1-5.2. No new PRD rewrite: the phase operationalizes what the PRD already promises (FR-15 marking honesty, FR-24 re-queue, NFR-8 freshness, "100% = exam readiness"); ADR-0001 amendments land with their owning epics, both stacks together (AD-14).

**Product constraints carried into every epic** (from the PRD/brief — non-negotiable at grill): evidence-only mastery, materials-sourced generation, plan-first IA, bilingual zh/en from the first ticket, self-directed study (gates order and certify — they never lock sequencing).
